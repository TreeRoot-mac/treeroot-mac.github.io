TreeRoot

TreeRoot transforms your macOS file system into an interactive tree visualization. Explore folders and files as a clear hierarchy—zoom, navigate, and understand your data structure without endless scrolling through flat lists.

Visual Hierarchy

Set any folder as root and define scan depth. Folders render as connected nodes with color-coded density, revealing crowded areas and item counts at a glance.

Flexible Layouts

Choose top-to-bottom, left-to-right, or other directions. Customize line styles—curved, straight, hierarchical—with adjustable colors, thickness, and density thresholds.

Smart Navigation

Click nodes for popovers with sortable contents by name, size, or date. Quick Look previews, direct app opens, and Finder integration keep your workflow seamless.

Precise Control

Toggle package folders like apps and bundles. Spot large or dense structures effortlessly, ideal for disk organization and rapid hierarchy traversal.

How It Works

1. Select Root

Choose any folder and set scan depth to build your tree.

2. Explore Visually

Navigate nodes, zoom in, and use layouts that match your style.

3. Interact Deeply

Click for previews, sorting, and quick actions in Finder or apps.

4. Refine and Analyze

Adjust visuals to highlight densities and uncover patterns.

FAQ

Is TreeRoot safe for my files?

TreeRoot only reads your file system structure—no modifications, just visualization.

What macOS versions are supported?

Optimized for macOS Sonoma and later, with native Apple Silicon support.

Can I customize the appearance?

Yes—layouts, lines, colors, and density rules adapt to your preferences.

Does it handle large drives?

Scan depth limits ensure smooth performance on any volume.
